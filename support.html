Common questions

How do reminders work?

PerkaBoo schedules reminders on your phone before each credit expires. By default they arrive at 9:00 AM: monthly credits 3 days before, quarterly and every-six-month credits 14 and 3 days before, and yearly credits 30, 7 and 1 days before. You can change the time and the timing in Settings, and with PerkaBoo Pro you can set timing for a single credit.

I am not getting reminders.

Check that notifications are allowed for PerkaBoo in your phone's Settings, and that a Focus mode or Do Not Disturb is not silencing them. Open the credit in PerkaBoo and make sure reminders are switched on for it. Credits you have already marked used or skipped do not send reminders. Opening the app also refreshes your reminders.

What is the "card anniversary" for?

Some credits reset on your card anniversary instead of the calendar year. PerkaBoo asks for the month you opened the card so it can work out each credit's period. Your calendar-based credits do not need it.

A credit on my card is wrong or has changed.

Issuers change their credits often. Please email us with the card and the credit, and we will check it. You can also add your own credits to any card, and you can edit the ones you add yourself.

Why are some credits marked "Automatic" or "Perk"?

Automatic credits are applied by the issuer without you doing anything, so PerkaBoo does not remind you about them or count them as unused value. Perks, such as a free night award, have no dollar value, so they get reminders but do not add to your unused total.

Is my data safe? What happens if I get a new phone?

Your data lives only on your phone. If your phone backup includes PerkaBoo, restoring from that backup brings your data back. PerkaBoo Pro includes a data export so you can keep a copy of your own. We cannot recover data for you, because we never receive it.

How do I restore or cancel PerkaBoo Pro?

To restore, open PerkaBoo, go to Settings and tap Restore purchases. To cancel, open your phone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose PerkaBoo. Refunds are handled by Apple.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app from your phone. Everything PerkaBoo stores is removed with it. To remove a single card, open it and choose Remove this card.
