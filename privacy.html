Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026 · Published by SJBApps

The short version. PerkaBoo keeps your card names and benefit tracking on your phone. We have no accounts and no servers, we never ask for card numbers or bank logins, and we do not sell or share your information or use it for advertising.

What stays on your device

When you use PerkaBoo, the following is stored only on your phone:

  • The cards you pick or add, and any nickname you give them.
  • The month of your card anniversary, if you enter one.
  • The credits you track, their schedules, and whether you marked each period used, partly used or skipped, including amounts and notes you type.
  • Your settings, such as the time of day and timing of reminders, and a saved copy of whether you have PerkaBoo Pro.

We cannot see this information. Deleting the app deletes it. If you use iCloud or computer backups for your phone, your phone's backup settings decide whether PerkaBoo's data is included in them.

What we never collect

  • Credit or debit card numbers, security codes or expiration dates.
  • Bank logins, account numbers, or transactions. PerkaBoo does not connect to your bank or cards.
  • Your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • An advertising identifier. PerkaBoo has no ads and does no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled by your phone itself, at the times you choose. They are not sent from a server. Allowing notifications is optional, and you can change it any time in your phone's Settings.

Subscriptions (PerkaBoo Pro)

Purchases are made through Apple's App Store, which handles your payment. Apple does not give us your payment details. To confirm whether you have PerkaBoo Pro, and to restore it on a new phone, we use a service called RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives a randomly generated anonymous app user ID, your purchase and subscription status from the App Store, and technical details such as your device type, operating system version, app version and IP address, which are part of any internet request. We do not send RevenueCat your cards, benefits or notes.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, it may send a crash report to Sentry so we can fix the problem. A report contains technical information such as the type of phone, the iOS version, the app version and where in the code the error happened. We configure it not to include personal information, and it does not include your cards, benefits or notes.

Exporting your data

PerkaBoo Pro lets you export your data as a file. The file is created on your phone and you choose where it goes, such as Files, AirDrop or Mail. We do not receive it.

Sharing

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising. The only outside services involved are the ones described above: Apple (purchases), RevenueCat (subscription status) and Sentry (crash reports).

Children

PerkaBoo is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Delete your data: delete the app from your phone.
  • Turn off reminders: change notifications for PerkaBoo in your phone's Settings, or mute a credit inside the app.
  • Cancel a subscription: in your phone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  • Questions or requests about information held by RevenueCat or Sentry: contact us and we will help you with them.

Changes to this policy

If we change how PerkaBoo handles information, for example by adding accounts or analytics, we will update this page and the effective date before the change reaches the app.
