Every credit, one place, one tap.

Premium cards come with dozens of credits that reset monthly, quarterly or every year. Miss one and the money is gone. PerkaBoo lists them all, groups them by what expires soon, and lets you mark each one used with a tap.

  • Pick from popular cards, or add your own
  • Reminders you control, per credit if you like
  • Catch up on the months before you started
  • See what you captured, and what slipped away

How it works

1

Pick your cards

Choose from a starter library with every credit, its amount and when it resets.

2

Get a nudge in time

A notification names the credit, the amount and the days left. You choose when.

3

Tap to mark it used

The next period starts by itself, and your history stays for the yearly summary.

Catch up on the year

Started mid-year? Mark what you already used so your totals are right.

Search everything

Find any credit by merchant, card or type, even on cards you do not have yet.

Favorites and perks

Star the credits you use most, and value extra perks like streaming and fuel savings.

See your progress

How much of the year's value you have captured, and what is left until December 31.

Private by design

Everything you enter stays on your phone. PerkaBoo has no accounts and no servers of its own, and it never asks for card numbers or bank logins. Read the privacy policy.

PerkaBoo Pro

The free plan covers two cards with all reminders. Pro adds unlimited cards, custom reminder timing for each credit, a yearly summary, extra perks with your own values, and data export. Subscriptions are handled by the App Store and can be cancelled any time.
