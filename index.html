Never lose a credit card perk.

PerkaBoo tracks every statement credit on your premium cards, reminds you before each one expires, and shows what you have captured. No bank connection, no account, no card numbers.

Pick your cards

Choose from a starter library of popular premium cards, or add your own. Every credit comes with its amount and how often it resets.

⏰

See what expires soon

Home groups your credits into this week, this month and later, with a running total of the value you have not used yet.

🔔

Get reminded in time

A notification names the credit, the amount and the days left. You choose when, and how early, per credit if you like.

✅

One tap to mark used

Mark a credit used, partly used or skipped. When a period ends, the next one starts automatically and the old one stays in your history.

What credits does your card have?

See every statement credit, its amount and when it resets, for popular premium cards. Start with the credits that expire December 31 or browse by card.

Private by design

Everything you enter stays on your phone. PerkaBoo has no accounts and no servers of its own, and it never asks for card numbers or bank logins. Read the privacy policy.

PerkaBoo Pro

The free plan covers two cards with all reminders. Pro adds unlimited cards, custom reminder timing for each credit, a yearly summary of the value you captured, and data export. Subscriptions are handled by the App Store and can be cancelled any time.
